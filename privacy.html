Privacy Policy

ZenBlocks · last updated 17 September 2026 · Data controller: Hugo Moriceau, France

The short version

ZenBlocks has no account, no sign-in and no server of mine. I receive no personal data from this app at all. Your board, your score and your tokens are written to storage on your own device and stay there.

What is stored, and where

  • The current board and the pieces in hand, saved after every placement
  • Your best score, current combo streak and token balance
  • Your sound, haptics and appearance settings
  • Whether the one-time ad-removal purchase has been made

All of it lives in the app's own storage on your device. It is included in an encrypted iPhone or iPad backup if you make one, and deleting the app deletes every bit of it.

Purchases

Removing ads, and any token pack, is sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your name, your payment method or your Apple Account. The app asks Apple's StoreKit whether a purchase exists on this device and receives yes or no.

Advertising, and the two prompts you will see

While ads are switched on they are supplied by Google AdMob, an independent advertising network whose own terms govern what it does with an ad request. Before any of that happens:

  • Apple's tracking prompt. The app asks for permission before the advertising identifier can be shared. Decline it and the ads you see are not personalised. Nothing in the game changes either way, and you can revisit the answer in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • A consent form in the EEA and the UK. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to obtain the consent GDPR requires before a personalised ad is requested. It can be reopened from the app's settings.

The app also carries Apple's SKAdNetwork identifiers, the mechanism by which the App Store attributes ad campaigns without disclosing who you are — Apple performs the attribution, and no user-level identifier reaches me.

Buying the ad removal ends this permanently: no further ad requests are made.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, the leaderboard is operated by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. It is optional, the game is complete without it, and nothing from it reaches me.

Analytics

There are none. No crash reporter, no attribution SDK, no telemetry, no product-analytics library. I do not know how many boards you have cleared. The only third-party code in the app is Google's advertising and consent libraries described above.

Children

ZenBlocks is not directed at children under 13, and collects no personal data from anyone of any age.

Your rights

The GDPR gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection over personal data a controller holds about you. I hold none from this app, so there is nothing to disclose or erase on my side; deleting the app erases everything on yours. If you would like that confirmed in writing, write to hugomoriceau@icloud.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and the new text is published here before the change ships in an update.
